# Game Studios and their proprietary engines

## Introduction

In this document we go through various game studio in-house proprietary game engines.

### Proprietary game engines

Some game studios from indie to mid sized to AAA use proprietary game engines to develop their games.

### In-house engines

- Northlight (Remedy Entertainment), Quantum Break, Max Payne, Alan Wake 2 and Control.
- Frostbite (EA), battlefield, Dead Space, Plants vs. Zombies Garden Warfare 2, Star Wars Battlefront I and II, Need for speed, Mirror's Edge Catalys, EA Sport Games, Mass Effect: Andromeda.

### Why they use in-house engines?

For instance Remedy uses their own Northlight game engines because it allows them to have full contorl over content pipelines and workflows, in addition to all the runtime capabilities. Game studios use in-house engines because they can advance the technology for the dev teams and for the games.

### Frostbite engine

Frostbite 1 game engine which made its debut in the 2008 video game Battlefield: Bad Company. The engine was developed with and HDR Audio and destruction 1.0 and as new games released Frostbite engine was upgraded according to their needs. Over the years of developing Frostbite engine its newest version is called just Frostbite droppping the version numbers.
