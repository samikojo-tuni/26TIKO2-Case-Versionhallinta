# Godot

## What is godot
**Godot** is a free and open-source game engine, that is constructed to make 2D and 3D games, with either its own coding language **GDScript** or other coding languages such as **C#** or **C++**.

Main platforms **Godot** supports are Desktop platforms(Linux, macOS, Windows), Web platform HTML5, android phones and tablets and virtual reality platforms like Meta quest.

Games can also be exported to additional console platforms through third-party companies like **W4 Games**, a commercial company founded by Godot coders.

## Notable features
Godot has multitude of features to make both 2D and 3D games, with seperate rendering systems for both graphich engines. Two graphigh engines can be also be mixed together with tools offered by godot.

Godot also has its own scripting language **GDScript**, that was made to be much simpler language for beginners, taking influence from **Python** language.
